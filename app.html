APP DESIGN

PROCESS FINAL

Description

Nightlife is a high-fidelity concept mobile app designed to help people safely navigate the Waterloo nightlife scene through club and venue recommendations combined with safety-focused features. As the Safety Features Developer, my role was to design the app’s safety tools, including alerts and information systems. The project addresses the problem of scattered, non-safety-focused nightlife information, especially for students and newcomers trying to make informed decisions about where to go and how to stay safe at night.
