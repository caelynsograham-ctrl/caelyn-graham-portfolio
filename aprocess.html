First Iteration

Our goal was to design a nightlife app tailored specifically to the Waterloo region. We recognized a lack of accessible and centralized information surrounding local clubs and nightlife, particularly for students who may be new to the social scene. With this in mind, we aimed to create a safe and reliable platform that users could turn to for guidance, planning, and connection, which led to the first iteration of our app, Night Life. During the design process, I was responsible for developing the app’s safety features. My focus was on creating tools that helped users stay connected while out at venues, encouraging groups to remain together and improving overall safety and communication throughout the nightlife experience.

Research & Usability Insights

Our main priority was fixing the parts of the app that disrupted the user experience and prevented smooth use of the nightlife app. While we had many additional ideas for improvements and clarification, time and feasibility limited what we could implement. Brainstorming solutions was easy, but actually designing even small fixes in Figma—like adding a cancel option for an accidentally selected venue—would add to the final result.
